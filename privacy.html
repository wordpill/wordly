Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2025

Wordly is a browser-based word counter tool that performs all text processing locally on your device. We do not send, store, process, or share your text content on any external server. All operations (including word/character counting, formatting, and saving) are executed entirely client-side (within your web browser).

Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, or access any text you enter into the Word Counter tool. The website may use standard web analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) and cookies to monitor anonymous traffic patterns, track service performance, and improve user experience. These analytics and cookies do not contain or access the content you paste into the tool. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Third-party Services

We may use third-party services (such as analytics tools or ad networks) to support the website. These third-party services have their own privacy policies, and we do not control their data practices. Importantly, these services do NOT access, collect, or process the text you enter into our Word Counter tool. When advertising is enabled, ad providers may collect anonymous usage data (e.g., device type, browsing behavior) in compliance with their own policies. We comply with Google AdSense data use policies. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your browser settings.
