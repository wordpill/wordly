About Wordly

Our mission is to provide fast, accurate, and privacy-friendly text counting tools — no login, no clutter, just what you need to stay focused. Wordly is a simple and free online word counter created to help students, writers and content creators check their text quickly. The tool runs entirely in your browser, and no text is sent to any server.

Features include real-time counts for words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs, plus handy utilities like one-click copy, text download, and quick formatting (lowercase/uppercase/trim spaces).

If you have feedback or suggestions to help us improve, please reach out anytime. You can contact us at support@wordly.app.
